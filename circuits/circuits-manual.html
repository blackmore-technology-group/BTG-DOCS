Availability and authorization: These are user responsibilities and intended operating procedures. They do not instruct readers to use an unqualified or unavailable production interface. Follow only the controls and permissions of a verified BTG release.

Who this manual is for

System owners, researchers, institutional evaluators and authorized nontechnical operators. Readers should first understand the product scope and respect the fact that roles, contracts, source permissions and approvals cannot be created by a mere software screen.

Before starting

  • Use only an official, current and authorized release.
  • Identify which organization or account is responsible for the action.
  • Review the permissions, data classification, allowed scope and supporting rights.
  • Verify current product availability and the limits of published evidence.
  • Do not disclose passwords, access tokens, financial details, sensitive source records or confidential algorithms.

01 — Read a DataChip record

Identify the capability, its owner, version, permitted inputs, output meaning, test scope and restrictions. Do not use a chip solely on the strength of its title.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

02 — Understand a Circuit

Trace each step in the published overview and note dependencies, points where decisions are made, data classifications and any human approvals.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

03 — Review a Genome revision

Compare version IDs, substituted components, tests required after the change, rights effects and whether release qualification was renewed.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

04 — Run a permitted composition

Only within an authorized product release, select a qualified configuration, confirm the permitted data scope, review diagnostics and retain the run reference.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

05 — Handle a failed run

Stop reuse of the affected result when validity is uncertain. Preserve the version, error category, bounded test evidence and any permitted diagnostic record for review.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

06 — Assess a commercial claim

Ask whether the right offered is software utility, transferable IP or a separately authorized investment. Examine what precisely is licensed and what is withheld.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

Understanding statuses and results

Proposed: a design or candidate, not an accepted product or offering. Qualified: supported by named evidence within a stated test scope. Authorized: a competent rights holder, organization or regulator has given the particular approval required. Completed: the independently defined outcome has actually occurred, including any external settlement or delivery where applicable.

Common questions

Does the public document grant access?

No. Publishing a description or a manual does not give readers access to private software, underlying data or investments.

Does a test pass establish production readiness?

No. Tests must be interpreted by version and scope; actual production readiness requires the applicable operational, legal and security qualifications.

Who decides data or investment rights?

The legally competent rights holder, issuer, contracting parties and applicable authorities—not a software identifier or automation alone.

Support and issue records

Record the date, relevant public identifier, product version if known, symptom or question, steps observed and the minimum permitted evidence. Report through BTG’s official contact page. Avoid attaching unrelated confidential or personal records.
