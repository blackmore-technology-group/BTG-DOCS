CORRECT CANONICAL COMPUTE MODEL · OCT 26, 2026

FarmNodes are logical DataChip/Circuit compute units — not physical computers

A Farm is an addressable assembly of logical DataChip and Circuit FarmNodes; a Farm family can define millions of reusable logical Farm instances without allocating one computer to each. Actual workloads run through the original ADAM/BTDU runtime on finite authorized physical Hosts. The three-machine engineering gate concerns three independent physical Hosts, not a three-FarmNode limit.

A local demonstration addressed 1,000,000 virtual Farms and 4,000,000 logical slots, executing four original Circuits and recording eight original Host receipts. This is not four million simultaneous jobs, independent new inventions, real settled trades or production host qualification.

Availability and authorization: These are user responsibilities and intended operating procedures. They do not instruct readers to use an unqualified or unavailable production interface. Follow only the controls and permissions of a verified BTG release.

Who this manual is for

Research sponsors, laboratory operators, institutions, communities and reviewers. Readers should first understand the product scope and respect the fact that roles, contracts, source permissions and approvals cannot be created by a mere software screen.

Before starting

  • Use only an official, current and authorized release.
  • Identify which organization or account is responsible for the action.
  • Review the permissions, data classification, allowed scope and supporting rights.
  • Verify current product availability and the limits of published evidence.
  • Do not disclose passwords, access tokens, financial details, sensitive source records or confidential algorithms.

01 — Describe a research request

State the question, legitimate purpose, permissible data, measurable acceptance criteria, time budget, reviewers and disallowed effects.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

02 — Select an approved laboratory

Check the domain, qualifications, current operator, resource status and independent-host evidence. Do not infer certification from a farm label.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

03 — Authorize a research run

Confirm the mandate and data permissions through the responsible authority before releasing information or computing resources.

04 — Read experiment results

Check what was observed, what failed, which versions ran, whether the work was reproduced independently and the limits of any success claim.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

05 — Challenge a finding

Submit a counterexample, alternate test or documented disagreement through the research review procedure; preserve both successful and adverse results.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

06 — Consider reuse or commerce

Separate qualified scientific usefulness from legal authority to licence or issue instruments. Request a rights record before claiming commercial entitlement.

Check before completion: Confirm the responsible authority, the object or record involved, the applicable rights, the intended outcome and any required human review. Preserve only the permitted evidence of the action.

Understanding statuses and results

Proposed: a design or candidate, not an accepted product or offering. Qualified: supported by named evidence within a stated test scope. Authorized: a competent rights holder, organization or regulator has given the particular approval required. Completed: the independently defined outcome has actually occurred, including any external settlement or delivery where applicable.

Common questions

Does the public document grant access?

No. Publishing a description or a manual does not give readers access to private software, underlying data or investments.

Does a test pass establish production readiness?

No. Tests must be interpreted by version and scope; actual production readiness requires the applicable operational, legal and security qualifications.

Who decides data or investment rights?

The legally competent rights holder, issuer, contracting parties and applicable authorities—not a software identifier or automation alone.

Support and issue records

Record the date, relevant public identifier, product version if known, symptom or question, steps observed and the minimum permitted evidence. Report through BTG’s official contact page. Avoid attaching unrelated confidential or personal records.

Detailed workflow and decision record

This is a task and review manual for a planned or specifically authorized deployment. Screen names, buttons, account permissions and automatic outcomes must be checked against the actual tested product version; where the UI has not been verified, instructions are deliberately expressed as actions, not invented clicks.

Frame question

Write practical objective, acceptable sources, failure criteria, timeframe and named sponsor.

Assign laboratory

Check farm identity, active host, data permissions, reviewer separation and computing limits.

Authorize experiment

Record scope, isolation rules, approved toolset and safe termination conditions.

Read results

Distinguish completion from scientifically validated performance and inspect failures.

Request challenge

Require separate tests with frozen candidate and known input provenance.

Consider economic release

Seek rights, usefulness and security review; no asset is automatically market-ready.

Before operating — acceptance checklist

  • Identify the actual operating version and service owner.
  • Confirm your role and explicit authority for this data, research or financial action.
  • Read the purpose, risks, permitted scope and available evidence before taking action.
  • Know which support and incident-reporting channel applies and how to stop safely.
  • Make no release, resale, rights-transfer or investment decision on a proposed record alone.

Expected workflow

  1. Create a research mandate and eligible domain
  2. Assign data permissions, constraints and budget
  3. Choose a logical farm and confirm real runtime identity
  4. Execute bounded tasks and retain failures
  5. Review evidence against external challenge cases
  6. Seek truly independent repeat tests before commercial promotion

Problem and recovery guide

What you noticeWhat to do
Unexpected network/data accessStop the run and retain controlled incident evidence.
Experiment exhausted its budgetMark incomplete; do not promote partial results.
Replicator disagreesRecord discrepancy, environment and exact result rather than hiding it.
“Independent node” on same PCCorrect the description to logical worker and leave physical independence unqualified.

What the operator should record

  1. Date, timezone, role and intended action.
  2. Public object identifier and version, never a secret or private credential.
  3. The authority, permission or review reference in appropriately protected storage.
  4. Expected outcome versus observed result, including negative and failed cases.
  5. Whether action is proposed, internally qualified, externally authorized or actually completed.
  6. Escalation destination and final decision, if any.

What this manual does not promise

Not every capability described here is available to a public account or installed in a consumer application. Real operation depends on qualified builds, actual provider permissions, formally delegated roles and sometimes legal authorization. Do not infer an available menu, released feature, verified rights entitlement or active securities market from a conceptual workflow.

Documents to request before relying on a result

  • Hardware/host enrollment and control attestations
  • Isolation, least-privilege and negative test results
  • Exact research-task and artifact identifiers
  • Separate evaluator acceptance criteria and observations
  • 3 independent host demonstrations if claimed
  • Rights and source-data permission records
