# HUNT3D — Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** Blackmore Technology Group Limited  
**Effective date:** November 9, 2026  
**Applies to:** HUNT3D mobile application for iOS and Android (Boundary Country pilot and subsequent releases)

HUNT3D is a three-dimensional outdoor navigation and habitat-analysis application. This notice explains how HUNT3D handles location, route, field-observation and device information. It is specific to HUNT3D; the company's broader website privacy information is available at [blackmoretechgroup.com/privacy.html](https://www.blackmoretechgroup.com/privacy.html).

## Location and device sensors

With your permission, HUNT3D accesses **precise location** while the app is in use to display your position on terrain, follow GPS, plan routes, assess local terrain and optionally record a track. Accuracy depends on the device and conditions. If permission is denied, location-dependent features will not work, but other map exploration may remain available. HUNT3D's current iOS configuration does **not** enable background location tracking.

With your permission, HUNT3D can use the device's compass, orientation and motion sensors to align the ground-level map. Sensor readings are used for navigation presentation. Do not rely on the app as a substitute for maps, field judgment or official access and safety notices.

## Routes, maps and field observations

HUNT3D can retain saved trips, route points, tracks, imported GPX/GeoJSON files, map preferences, and optional wildlife or habitat field-observation records on your device. Such records may include coordinates, species, survey metadata, evidence references and timestamps you enter. The current field-observation workflow does not automatically upload those records.

You control deliberate exports or sharing outside HUNT3D. Exported files, copies and backups are governed by the location and services to which you choose to send them.

## Internet connections and third-party services

To supply weather, map or land-reference information, HUNT3D may request data from third-party public services, including Environment and Climate Change Canada and British Columbia geographic-data services. Requests may contain a map-area bounding box or location associated with the area you are viewing. These services, and the network providers involved, may receive technical request information such as an IP address, request time and requested region, and apply their own privacy practices. A displayed map position may be based on your GPS position if you choose location-following features.

The app may download terrain, map and regional content. Download requests can generate ordinary hosting or network logs. The app does not present wildlife predictions as observed live animal locations.

## In-app purchases and payments

The Boundary Country pilot currently has no enabled in-app map purchases. HUNT3D includes components for possible future region purchases; when any purchase feature is enabled, relevant transactions and entitlements may be processed by Apple, Google and an app-store purchase service such as RevenueCat under their respective policies. The publisher does not receive full payment-card details from Apple or Google. The policy and App Store disclosures should be updated before enabling new purchase or analytics functions.

## How we use and disclose information

Location and optional field records are used to deliver map navigation, route tracking, local terrain context and habitat analysis. HUNT3D does not require a BTG account for the currently described Boundary pilot.

We do not intentionally sell users' precise GPS trails or private field-observation records. We do not publicly post your saved routes or field records merely because you use the app. Technical network requests to map, weather and app-delivery providers are described above. If the app is expanded with new online services, the notice and relevant store disclosures will be updated.

## Retention and deletion

Locally saved information remains on the device until you delete it through an available in-app control, clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to operating-system backups and files you previously exported. Uninstalling the app does not delete copies you exported to other apps or cloud services.

For questions about a server-side record or deletion request, contact BTG using the method below and describe the type of record without sending sensitive hunting locations unnecessarily.

## Security and safety

BTG aims to limit data collection to what the relevant function needs. Any network communication can carry ordinary technical metadata. No GPS, compass, trail, crossing, access or habitat prediction is guaranteed accurate or safe. Verify land access, hunting regulations, weather, closures and trail conditions with authoritative sources before field use.

## Children

HUNT3D is intended for adult outdoor and hunting-related activities, not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this notice when app functions, third-party services or data practices change. The current version and effective date will be shown here. Material changes will be reflected in the relevant App Store privacy disclosures.
