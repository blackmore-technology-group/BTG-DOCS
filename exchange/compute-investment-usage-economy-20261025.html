Compute Contribution, Technology Use & Value

Owner-approved distinction between participating with real computing work, licensing technology and valuing actual production.

Current design and dated test evidence. Research acceptance is not full production qualification.

Investment is contribution of real working resources

Under the current owner-defined compute-participation design, eligible contributors can make actual authorized CPU, GPU, memory or other processing resources available for useful work. Contractually specified participation can be considered only against independently measured, attributable completed contribution and the applicable rights contract. A device connection or declared memory number alone does not equal an investment.

Technology use produces the service transaction

In this model, one valid authorized execution/use event—bound to a real technology version, permitted service right, qualified FarmNode device, and uniquely auditable execution receipt—can be treated as one idempotent technology-use transaction. Requested two-million uses are a hypothetical demand scenario, not two-million verified trades.

Economic value is production, not imaginary share pricing

Technology asset assessment must use verified license usage, independently reconciled real revenues, actual costs, utilization and operating economics. An unissued proposed ceiling of 1,000,000 participation units is not a million dollars in investment and cannot be multiplied by a fictional last traded price to imply market capitalization. Conventional securities remain a separate instrument family; data use, transferable IP/revenue rights and investment rights must be labelled separately.

How the five systems participate

ENTITY: verifiable owner, contributor, user and asset identity and authorization. Bridge: safe data transfer and authorized grant challenge without unrelated personal records. FarmNode: genuinely executes permitted units of work and creates measured receipts. BTG Studio: constructs compatible DataChips and products with reproducible versions. Bit Trade Global: represents permitted rights and reconciles authorized use and contractual economic participation, subject to rules of each instrument.
