Three different transactions

Use right (U): a licence to use a defined asset for a stated purpose, term and scope. Transferable asset right (A): an assignment or transfer of an identified intellectual-property or commercial interest, only to the extent legally granted. Investment security (I): an ownership, debt or other legally structured financial interest in an issuer; it does not automatically confer software execution or dataset access.

How computing is authorized

1. The account authenticates and proves control of the relevant right or grant. 2. The instrument or separate computing-entitlement record identifies a specific licensed circuit or capability. 3. ENTITY verifies subject, device, issuer, revocation and permitted scope. 4. Bridge delivers only consented inputs. 5. Studio supplies the permitted exact circuit version. 6. FarmNode verifies limits and deduplicates submissions. 7. The execution result includes a signed receipt with the correct job, identity and version. No step may be skipped because a ticker exists.

Required instrument information

Every instrument should disclose identifier and issuer, origin/provenance, legal classification, exact rights and exclusions, versioned eligible capability, duration and limits, revocation and dispute provisions, price and fee information when applicable, dependencies and risks. Instrument information pages are not substitutes for enforced permissions.

Current engineering

A local Studio integration was added to check account, identity, device, scope, version, compute limits and duplicate jobs. Its associated eight tests had two errors as of 24 October. The original Exchange's 221-test backend regression passed, but separate real purchases, entitlement redemption, cross-device authentication, payment settlement and live financial trading were not demonstrated by those tests.

Investor and user distinction

Someone who invests in an asset-focused company might own a security tied economically to that issuer. They do not automatically own the underlying data or obtain unlimited access to run a model. A licensed operator might hold compute rights without being an investor. These differences must be shown before a person makes a purchase or enters into a licence.
