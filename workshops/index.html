Session structure and practical promise

At least two-thirds of each workshop is hands-on building, testing and demonstrating. Short explanations lead to creating something runnable, then participants test what happened, fix a problem and save a checkpoint. Learners can use START, BUILD and optional EXPLORE challenge cards; advancement is supportive rather than competitive.

Safety and privacy safeguards

  • No public child profiles, unsupervised chat, advertising, microtransactions, real-money experiences or automatic public sharing.
  • No child projects or personal information are transferred into BTG commercial research systems, Bridge or Bit Trade Global Exchange.
  • Guardian approval is separate for attendance, photographs, video and distribution; the default showcase is private without recording.
  • Volunteers must be screened as required by the actual host organization and taught conduct, emergency and incident procedures.
  • Avoid real emergency dispatch, field robotics, drones, high-voltage equipment, soldering and unsafe vehicle/public infrastructure interactions.
  • Accessible devices, adjustable pace, breaks, quiet options and alternative input are planned.
  • Use de-identified progress measures rather than public ranking or profiling children.

Approvals required before scheduling or enrolment

  1. Named host and programme coordinator accept governance responsibilities.
  2. Screening, incident response, first aid expectations, supervision ratios and insurance are approved.
  3. Room, equipment, accessibility and offline recovery are actually tested.
  4. Child/guardian permissions, privacy notices, attendance procedures and safe storage are approved.
  5. Facilitator dry-run and full eight-week lesson packs meet the stated acceptance checklist.
  6. Only then publish real dates, prices, confirmed venue, registrations or partner names.
