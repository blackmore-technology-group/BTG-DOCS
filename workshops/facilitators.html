Facilitator readiness and safety

  1. Confirm a responsible host, approved safeguarding lead, ratios and screening under applicable policy.
  2. Preflight 12 learner stations plus two spares, offline projects and recovery tools.
  3. Check room exits, labelled power cables, accessible inputs, quiet space and low-voltage kits.
  4. Prepare START / BUILD / EXPLORE challenge cards for the assigned age level.
  5. Read consent and media restrictions: no personal child data in project files or public platforms.
  6. Provide a brief demonstration, followed by hands-on making for at least two-thirds of time.
  7. End with the learner testing, saving a version and privately reflecting.
  8. Record incidents and attendance in host-controlled storage only.

Eight-session delivery and challenge levels

WeekCore milestoneWhat evidence to retain privately
1Imagine and startSaved version, observed test and reflection: Choose an original safe project, draw the main screen or build target, complete first runnable interaction.
2Build the world/interfaceSaved version, observed test and reflection: Create primary scene or home screen; set intended start and finish.
3Control and responseSaved version, observed test and reflection: Connect events, controls, buttons or sensors to observable actions.
4Rules and objectivesSaved version, observed test and reflection: Add logic, a useful task or goal; test both success and failure behaviour.
5Feedback and accessibilitySaved version, observed test and reflection: Add instructions, sound/visual feedback and suitable input alternatives.
6Find and fix defectsSaved version, observed test and reflection: Run a peer or mentor test, record unexpected behaviour and repair at least one issue.
7Prepare a versionSaved version, observed test and reflection: Save named final candidate, perform restore/export test and create accessible credits.
8Private showcaseSaved version, observed test and reflection: Demonstrate to approved guests without default child identification or public upload.

Incident and consent boundaries

Never publish attendee names, photos, attendance reports or evaluations in the public BTG library. Handle conduct, injury, unsafe hardware, cyber incidents and privacy complaints using host-approved escalation and retention procedures. The workshop companion app is proposed, not a verified deployed system; paper checklists remain a valid fallback.
