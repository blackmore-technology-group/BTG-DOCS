Level 4 — Youth Innovators

A complete eight-session pathway for ages 15–17: projects, teaching steps, evidence, tools and child-safety boundaries.

What this level is designed to teach

Youth Innovators is the proposed programme for 15–17 years, with 90–120 minutes guided sessions. Teaching priorities: Independent project planning, versioning, peer review. By the end, the learner should be able to present a project such as polished windows game; utility app; modular robot, and explain at least one problem they solved.

Allowed tools and safety boundaries

Godot with optional GDScript, prototyping tools, version control concepts and supervised robotic state machines. No public child profiles or real emergency systems.

Finished project acceptance

  • Runnability on the approved workshop device.
  • An original child-made idea or improvement that the learner can explain.
  • At least one recorded or explained test, including what was expected.
  • One adjustment made because of feedback or a defect.
  • Local safe project packaging and only opt-in guardian-approved private sharing.

Facilitator and guardian notes

Age is a default placement guide, not a test of worth or ability. The host and parent can choose accommodations, quiet presentation and slower pace. A workshop is not independent childcare, a credit-bearing qualification or a commercial data contribution. The original blueprint is a proposal: there are no confirmed dates, named registered students or verified screening outcomes to publish.
