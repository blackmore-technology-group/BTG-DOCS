Publication correction policy

Superseded numbers or test descriptions should be labelled as historic, never quietly reused as current. Private source can remain private while public claims are individually checkable through signed, redacted receipts or controlled evaluations. The absence of a publicly inspectable receipt means the public description should be treated as a claim, not a verified independent proof.
