Regulatory assessment pathway

A structured regulatory and privacy review index, identifying applicable decision points without claiming recognition or approval.

Scope and legal classification come before product language

For each business activity, identify the actual entity, jurisdictions, prospective customer or investor, economic substance of what is transferred, custody model and payment flows. Neither “DCO”, “exchange”, “data share”, “ledger proof” nor “licence” determines regulatory treatment. The applicable regulator and qualified counsel must assess actual activities and securities classifications. This public document is an assessment index, not a regulatory filing or assertion of permission.

Regulatory evidence file checklist

  1. Named legal entity and accountable control persons; versioned functional business description.
  2. Jurisdiction/target-client and security-classification memorandum prepared by counsel.
  3. Data flow maps, privacy notices, contract and Indigenous data-sovereignty analysis where relevant.
  4. Marketplace/dealer/custody analysis with citations to current rules and regulator contact records.
  5. Architecture and segregation of personal, research, trading and issuer records.
  6. Threat model and independent security testing with scope, date, assessor and exceptions.
  7. Issue and incident management, conflicts, market abuse prevention, complaint handling and retention.
  8. Financial condition, client-funds custody, reconciliation, settlements and independent audit evidence.
  9. Release gates tying representations in consumer pages to actual authorizations.

Current evidenced status and immediate non-conclusions

Local engineering tests exist for several components. They do not establish independent three-host qualification, production account/connector privacy controls, compliant financial services, live securities issuance or recognized public market operations. The system is a project for technical and legal evaluation. Do not infer that the British Columbia Securities Commission, Canadian Securities Administrators, CIRO or another regulator has approved BTG.
