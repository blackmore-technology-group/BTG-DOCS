Understand BTG in everyday language

A jargon-light, practical guide to BTG, Bridge, research, data rights, and what the Exchange does—and does not—do.

Edition 2.0 · 23 October 2026 · Educational/public explanatory documentation · No public investment offering or approval claimed

Start with the question: What is BTG trying to build?

Think about an invention that helps a workshop diagnose equipment breakdowns. The knowledge in the invention may be useful for years. Traditionally, its economic value is usually wrapped inside a company or a one-off software licence. BTG is proposing a more visible record of the particular asset, the rights that may legitimately be sold or licensed, the evidence of usefulness and, potentially, a separately issued investment security connected to a business built around that asset.

The project connects specialized tools, each with one job. NIKI investigates solutions. FarmNodes run controlled experiments. DataChips and Circuits preserve reusable computations. BTDU retains authoritative knowledge. ENTITY identifies and documents allowed rights. ADAM governs approved changes. Bridge is a separate doorway for permissioned data from people and organizations. Bit Trade Global is the proposed market and instrument information layer.

A complete example without jargon

A company has a lawful right to a dataset showing how robots recover from mistakes. Its engineers develop a method that learns useful recovery patterns. A research laboratory tests the method, records failures and repeats the results. The asset owner decides what rights it is legally able to offer. A commercial customer might buy limited use. Separately, a corporation devoted to this asset could in theory seek approval to issue its own shares. The share buyer would invest in that corporation; they would not own the private records in the dataset.

What changes for an ordinary person?

Bridge could someday let an adult choose whether specified, lawfully available information contributes to permitted research. Connecting an account does not grant BTG ownership or authorize the Exchange to sell the contents. Nothing in the design guarantees payment for contribution.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy data shares now?

No live authorised investment offering or secondary trading venue has been evidenced by the public project documentation. Illustrative listings are not investable securities.

Does BTG own my information if I use Bridge?

Connection and consent are different from ownership or transferable economic rights. The actual agreement, platform rules and privacy law control permissible actions.

Will a computer invent things by itself?

NIKI is designed to propose, test and improve methods; tests reported to date are bounded and include failures. General autonomous invention has not been independently demonstrated as a released service.

Are Boundary Tech Generation workshops part of a data marketplace?

No. The youth workshops are separately proposed education activities. Children’s projects and personal information must not feed BTG commercial research or investment systems.

Before believing a claim

  1. Look for a dated version and a named test or external decision.
  2. Check whether the claim describes an intention, local test, independent validation, authorised operation or real-world result.
  3. Check what the customer or investor actually receives by law.
  4. Ask which facts remain unknown and whether any independent third party has verified them.
