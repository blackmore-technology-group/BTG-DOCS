Document request register

  1. Formal legal entity and shareholder/control documentation appropriate to the activity.
  2. Signed classification memorandum mapping each U/A/I instrument class to enforceable economic rights.
  3. Updated legal and regulatory requirements by Canada/BC and every additional operating jurisdiction.
  4. Corporate and per-issuer financial statements and transaction assumptions reconciled to actual records.
  5. Conflicts of interest, market abuse prevention, surveillance and participant complaint policies.
  6. Cybersecurity/penetration and privacy assessments under named independent scope.
  7. Release management showing which features remain disabled until legal or technical gate completion.
  8. Internal responsibility matrix, escalation contacts, record retention and revision control.
